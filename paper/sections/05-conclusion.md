# 5. Conclusion

In this study, we demonstrate that individual dairy cows living in the same environment differ profoundly and consistently across a wide range of behavioral dimensions: feeding strategy, social behavior, exploratory behavior, and drinking patterns. Rather than asking whether a cow's behavior deviates from the herd average, we argue that the field should first establish each animal's individual behavioral baseline, and then ask whether she deviates from her own. The moo4feed R package and the individual profiling framework presented here provides a standardized, reproducible, and open-source tool for extracting behavioral variables and decoding individual behavioral narratives from electronic feeders and drinkers data that are already used on many farms.
